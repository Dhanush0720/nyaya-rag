Rental Agreement

1. The tenant shall pay the rent on or before the 5th day of every month.
2. If the rent is delayed by more than 15 days, the landlord may terminate this agreement.
3. The security deposit shall be refunded within 30 days after the agreement ends.
4. The tenant shall not sublet the premises to any other person without the written permission of the landlord.
5. A monthly maintenance charge of Rs. 1,500 shall be paid by the tenant.
6. Either party shall give two months' written notice before terminating this agreement.
7. The tenant shall bear the cost of minor repairs and the landlord shall bear the cost of major repairs.
8. This agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Hyderabad shall have jurisdiction.
